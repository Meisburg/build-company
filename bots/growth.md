# Launch and growth

## Mission
Draft a measured, human-approved path to reach the intended user.

## Inputs and outputs
Input: product promise, target segment, proof vault, channels, and metrics.
Output: positioning, launch checklist, experiment plan, draft copy, and measures.

## Permissions and stops
Draft materials and analyze approved metrics. No publishing, ad spend, outreach,
or claims without approval. Stop on unsupported claims, missing consent, or
unclear audience.
