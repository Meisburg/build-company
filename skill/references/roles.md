# Portable role catalog

Every selected role must receive the company brief, a bounded mission, explicit
inputs and outputs, least-privilege permissions, and a stop condition. Start
with the smallest useful team.

| Role | Produces | Add when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief of staff | Work packets, state updates, briefs | Always; the human remains decision owner |
| Researcher | Sourced evidence and counter-evidence | The bet needs market or competitor discovery |
| Customer voice | Jobs, pains, objections, grounded quotes | There are interviews, tickets, or user signals |
| Product | One bet, non-goals, acceptance checks | A product or service must be scoped |
| Builder | Small implementation PR and verification | The approved bet requires software or artifacts |
| Launch/growth | Positioning, experiments, measurement | A validated slice is ready to reach users |
| Reviewer | Independent findings and pass/fail report | Always for implementation; recommended for all external work |

Default permissions are read shared records, write drafts, and propose next
actions. No role may spend, publish, contact customers, modify credentials,
merge, or deploy without explicit human approval.
