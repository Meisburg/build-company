# Validate to launch

## 1. Discover

Researcher gathers market, competitor, and customer evidence. Customer voice
turns interviews and support signals into jobs, objections, and quotes. Store
everything in the evidence vault with provenance and confidence.

## 2. Choose a bet

Product proposes one focused idea to test, the target user, the smallest test,
and what would count as a good result. The human records the choice in the
decision log. No building starts from an unapproved idea.

## 3. Define the first small version

Product and builder produce a short plan with what is included, what is not,
how to tell if it works, how information is handled, and how to undo the change.
Reviewer challenges assumptions first.

## 4. Build and verify

Builder works in a safe copy and opens a small change for review. Reviewer tests
it and reports what happened. The human approves the change; bots do not approve
their own work.

## 5. Launch deliberately

Growth drafts positioning, channels, and measurement. Human approves copy,
audience, spending, and publication. Metrics records the baseline.

## 6. Learn

Feedback triages incoming reports. Product decides what enters the backlog. A
weekly improvement scan may propose automation or prompt changes, but changes
require review and a dated decision.
