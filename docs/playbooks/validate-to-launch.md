# Validate to launch

## 1. Discover

Researcher gathers market, competitor, and customer evidence. Customer voice
turns interviews and support signals into jobs, objections, and quotes. Store
everything in the evidence vault with provenance and confidence.

## 2. Choose a bet

Product proposes one narrow hypothesis, target user, smallest test, and success
threshold. The human records the choice in the decision log. No build work
starts from an unapproved hypothesis.

## 3. Define the MVP

Product and builder produce a short plan with explicit non-goals, acceptance
checks, data handling, and rollback. Reviewer challenges assumptions first.

## 4. Build and verify

Builder works in an isolated branch and opens a small PR. Reviewer runs checks
and reports evidence. The human merges; bots do not self-approve their own work.

## 5. Launch deliberately

Growth drafts positioning, channels, and measurement. Human approves copy,
audience, spending, and publication. Metrics records the baseline.

## 6. Learn

Feedback triages incoming reports. Product decides what enters the backlog. A
weekly improvement scan may propose automation or prompt changes, but changes
require review and a dated decision.
